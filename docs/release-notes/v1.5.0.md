### 新增规则

#### 禁用僵尸围城 (`disableZombieSieges`)

禁止村庄僵尸围城生成，同时保持普通生物生成、僵尸增援、袭击及其他生成机制不变。

#### 移除悬浮挖掘惩罚 (`noAirborneMiningPenalty`)

移除玩家未接触地面时受到的挖掘速度惩罚；水下挖掘及其他挖掘速度修正保持原版。

#### 深板岩切石 (`stonecutterCutsDeepslate`)

Minecraft `1.17+` 可让深板岩在切石机中使用深板岩圆石可用的全部切石配方。

#### 混凝土粉末烧制玻璃 (`smeltConcretePowderToGlass`)

允许在普通熔炉中将 16 种颜色的混凝土粉末烧制为对应颜色的染色玻璃。

#### 蜜蜂免疫水伤害 (`noBeeWaterDamage`)

Minecraft `1.15+` 使蜜蜂接触或浸泡在水中时不再受到溺水伤害，同时保留水体物理、AI 与其他原版行为。

#### 经验修补自修复 (`mendingAbsorbXp`)

生存玩家副手持有受损且带有经验修补附魔的物品时，可周期性消耗玩家已储存经验进行修复；每消耗 1 点经验最多恢复 2 点耐久。

### New Rules

#### Disable Zombie Sieges (`disableZombieSieges`)

Prevents village zombie sieges while leaving normal mob spawning, zombie reinforcements, raids, and other spawning mechanics unchanged.

#### No Airborne Mining Penalty (`noAirborneMiningPenalty`)

Removes the mining-speed penalty applied while the player is not on the ground. Underwater mining and all other mining-speed modifiers remain vanilla.

#### Stonecut Deepslate (`stonecutterCutsDeepslate`)

On Minecraft `1.17+`, allows deepslate to use every stonecutting recipe available to cobbled deepslate.

#### Smelt Concrete Powder to Glass (`smeltConcretePowderToGlass`)

Allows all 16 colors of concrete powder to be smelted in a normal furnace into the corresponding stained glass.

#### No Bee Water Damage (`noBeeWaterDamage`)

On Minecraft `1.15+`, prevents bees from taking drowning damage from water while preserving water physics, AI, and other vanilla behavior.

#### Mending Absorbs Stored XP (`mendingAbsorbXp`)

When a Survival player holds a damaged Mending item in the offhand, the item periodically consumes stored player experience for repair. Each XP repairs up to 2 durability.

### GCA Fake-Player Inventory Enhancement

Adds an optional DDS client enhancement on top of GCA `openFakePlayerInventory`:

- GCA remains authoritative for the feature semantics and server-only compatibility;
- clients without a compatible DDS build continue to use the original GCA interface;
- compatible DDS clients can use the enhanced 77-slot interface;
- fake-player equipment, the contiguous 36-slot fake inventory, and the viewer inventory keep separate container identities;
- attack/use interval and continuous controls, stop, drop, disconnect, and fake hotbar selection are available;
- GCA's 8-block validity distance and sneak-right-click ownership are preserved;
- early GCA builds using the detached shadow `SimpleContainer` model fail closed to the original GCA UI.

The enhancement layer covers Minecraft `1.19.2–26.2` without making the DDS client a hard requirement for GCA.

### Undo/Redo Improvements

Further improves the Creative-only Undo/Redo system on Minecraft `1.21.9+`:

- expands recording across containers, hoppers, hopper minecarts, crafters, TNT minecarts, powered rails, and related interactions;
- strengthens ownership propagation through Scheduled Ticks, Block Events, redstone chains, and delayed consequences;
- improves cross-dimension entity handling and asynchronous TNT-related consequences;
- immediately releases runtime history and async state when the rule is disabled;
- keeps the complete server-only `/undo` and `/redo` workflow;
- optional DDS client shortcuts remain `Ctrl+Z` for Undo and `Ctrl+Alt+Z` for Redo.
